Audix Music Player

Version 2.0.0

Audix is a modern, browser-based music player designed for audiophiles who love control. Built with vanilla JavaScript and the Web Audio API, it delivers powerful equalization, intelligent lyrics fetching, and a gamified listening experience.

Developed by Utilities Nepal © 2026.

Privacy Policy

Local-First Architecture: Audix processes all audio files locally in your browser. Your music never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the radio streaming feature.

Data Storage: We use your browser's IndexedDB and localStorage to save your library, achievements, and custom radio stations. This data is not transmitted to any server.

Lyrics Fetching: When you request lyrics, only the song title and artist name are sent to LRCLIB's public API. No personal data is included.

No Tracking: Audix does not use cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party advertising scripts.
